# Crew Health interface

Operate mode. A flat white working surface on a pale cool ground, dark navy ink, Source Sans 3, and restrained teal for the main chart and actions. Amber signals attention and text always states the meaning. No decorative images or entrance animations. Touch targets at least 44px. A compact top navigation replaces a sidebar to favour tablet use. The signature element is the joint timeline of measurement context and crew events. The screen answers: what do we know now, what is unknown, and who has taken over?

Presenter controls live in an inline Demo setup panel opened from the visible synthetic-data notice. Scenario descriptions explain the expected starting state. Startup behavior and restore defaults use explicit text, with successful starts returning keyboard focus to the crew-status heading. Advanced replay controls remain below the main crew workflow.

Daily self-check follows the human handoff banner and precedes measurements, with five labelled rating choices and explicit discomfort answers. Saved cards collapse to a receipt and next-due indication. Low reports lead to the existing symptom form with local context rather than an assumed symptom. Signal Watch uses one large fixed response target, explicit WAIT/SIGNAL text, a separate practice path, and persistent reasons/metrics after completion. No health score, competitive ranking or reassuring green result is displayed.

Today places the human concern before the measurement overview. A single primary action opens reporting or handoff. The measurement explorer has a selector and one detailed trend, rather than seven competing charts. Common symptoms use large checkbox targets; additional symptoms, notes and manual readings are disclosed on demand. Unknown, historical, context-dependent and comparison states use explicit text. Detailed research and technical JSON belong in the ground-review flow.

Historical inspection uses a teal crosshair and selected-point outline, a native range scrubber and 44px step controls. Persistent details sit below the plot so touch users can read them without holding a finger in place. The selected historical observation remains separate from the current measurement summary. Selection never interpolates across gaps or changes the replay clock.
